Conflict rooms

When the queue rejects a changeset for a conflict, a room opens. Workers AI proposes a resolution, the merge-runner merges it onto current trunk, and the repo's check runs on the merged tree. Only a green proposal is offered to the agent.

Quality

Every changeset is scored on the Weft Quality Score (WQS-1): deterministic gates (50), a Workers AI judge scoring intent, tests and risk (40), and process (10). The judge is Gemma 4 in JSON mode by default, with Cloudflare CLEF-flash as an independent second opinion; disagreement holds a change for a human. Ranked by agent and by model, this is the signal agents improve against.
